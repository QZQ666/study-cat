# 三花猫“小花”素材

`calico-atlas.png` 是三花猫使用的透明背景动作图集，由内置 `imagegen` 工具生成并做过一次边缘清理编辑。程序按姿态裁切，在运行时缓存，并把图集作为 `StudyCat.CalicoAtlas` 资源嵌入 `StudyCat-Pets.exe`。新增布偶猫的素材及提示词见 [RAGDOLL.md](RAGDOLL.md)，两只猫可在主界面切换。

12 个姿态按行排列：坐着、闭眼眨眼、歪头、舔爪；左侧蜷睡、放松蜷睡、翻肚皮、右侧蜷睡；伸懒腰、打哈欠、睡梦伸爪、趴在书边。

图集的睡眠一行较矮，裁切参数与这份最终图集对应。替换成其他布局时，需要同步更新 `CalicoAssets.Load()` 中的行边界，避免带入相邻姿态。

## 初次生成提示词

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: a production-ready transparent sprite atlas for a Windows desktop study companion, ONE atlas image with exactly 4 columns x 3 rows, equal rectangular cells, ideal canvas 2048 x 1536 (4:3). A BEAUTIFUL CONSISTENT FEMALE CALICO CAT in all 12 cells, refined charming hand-painted storybook / premium cozy game illustration, soft detailed white fur with natural ginger-orange and charcoal-black patches, pink ears and tiny pink nose, fine whiskers, amber-green gentle eyes, fluffy rounded elegant silhouette, no clothes, no collar, no accessories except a small book in the last cell. Keep IDENTICAL distinctive markings across every pose: ginger patch around left ear/forehead, black patch around right ear, white muzzle/chest/paws, orange and black patches on back, fluffy ringed tail. Fully rendered delicate fur strokes, subtle warm shading, believable feline anatomy, attractive face, cute but not crude geometric cartoon, clean alpha edges, suitable for display at 210 pixels wide. TRUE transparent background, no scenery, no checkerboard, no text, no labels, no borders, no watermarks, no drawn tile lines. Each cat must be fully contained inside its own equal cell with generous transparent margins, horizontally centered, baseline near 84% of cell height, no overlaps between cells. Row-major order EXACTLY: Row1 col1 sitting awake looking gently at viewer; col2 same sitting pose eyes closed mid-blink; col3 sitting head tilted inquisitively; col4 sitting licking a raised front paw, visible small pink tongue. Row2 col1 curled sleeping on side, head left, tail around body, peaceful closed eyes; col2 SAME curled sleeping pose with a subtly softer relaxed chest for alternate breathing frame; col3 sleeping belly-up, paws loosely curled, showing soft white tummy as if rolling over; col4 curled sleeping with head pointing right, paws tucked. Row3 col1 long full-body stretch, front paws forward and rump slightly up; col2 sitting with an adorable small yawn; col3 curled sleeping with one paw slightly extended as a dream twitch; col4 awake resting forepaws on a tiny cream open book. All twelve poses are the SAME calico cat, keep render quality, scale and lighting consistent. The first eight cells are simple cat only with no objects. Do not add shadow backgrounds or pillows. Design each as an isolated transparent animation pose, not a collage of unrelated cats.

## 编辑提示词

Use case: precise-object-edit / background-extraction. Edit target: attached calico cat animation atlas. ONLY clean the cutout matte and fur edges. Remove all saturated bright red/yellow colored speckles, white jagged halos and stray pixels OUTSIDE the cats. Retain fine natural fur alpha edges. Background must be genuinely fully transparent. Preserve the original beautiful calico character, natural orange/black/white fur colors, facial identity, ALL TWELVE EXACT poses, the tiny book, fine fur detail, and especially the EXACT ORIGINAL composition, positions, sizes, row heights, column positions and canvas dimensions. Do not redesign or move anything. Do not add borders, outlines, shadows, text or objects. Clean professional transparent sprite edges, no colored fringe when composited over a dark or light desktop. Preserve every existing cat's original boundaries and layout; this is a precise matte cleanup only.
